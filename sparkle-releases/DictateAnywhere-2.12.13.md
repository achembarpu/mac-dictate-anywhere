## Microphone reliability

- Automatically choose an available microphone when a selected device disconnects or the MacBook lid is closed, while preserving your preferred microphone for when it becomes available again.
- Use the same capture path for automatic and manual microphone selection. Recording now waits for audio buffers before showing that it is listening, and retries another available input if capture does not start.
- Rename System Default to Automatic to describe the fallback behavior clearly.
- Remove iPhone and iPad Continuity microphones from microphone selection to avoid their activation delay.
