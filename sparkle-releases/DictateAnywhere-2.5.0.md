# Dictate Anywhere 2.5.0

- Added fully on-device Mandarin Chinese (Simplified) dictation with SenseVoice, Multilingual Nemotron, and Apple Speech on supported Macs.
- Chinese dictation now preserves natural punctuation and mixed Chinese-English terms more accurately.
- Improved Chinese text insertion and filler-word cleanup, including safer handling of words whose meaning could be changed by over-aggressive cleanup.
- Long recordings now preserve intentional repeated phrases at transcription chunk boundaries.
- Updated FluidAudio to 0.15.5. Multilingual Nemotron requires Apple Silicon; SenseVoice remains available on Intel Macs with a compatible model.
