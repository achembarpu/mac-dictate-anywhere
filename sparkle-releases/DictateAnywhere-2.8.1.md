# Dictate Anywhere 2.8.1

- S1-mini cleanup now adapts its trained styling to Email, Work Messaging, Personal Messaging, and Other app categories.
- S1-mini's Automatic context uses its trained Email or General value without sending surrounding field text to the model or changing its prompt format.
- Transcript Cleanup now shows only the prompt, vocabulary, context, and writing controls that the selected cleanup method can use.
- Local Ollama and OpenAI-compatible servers receive local context without showing an irrelevant remote-sharing switch.
- The menu-bar custom-vocabulary shortcut is hidden whenever the selected cleanup method cannot use custom vocabulary.
